Speakoff Privacy Policy

Version 4 · Effective 26 November 2026

Speakoff helps you practise speaking a language: you record answers, our AI scores them, and you can play Ranked matches against other players. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, how long we keep it, how we protect it, and the rights you have. We collect only what the app needs.

Where a law requires a local representative or domestic agent (for example in the EU/UK or Korea), we will name them here before offering the service there.

3. How your speech is analysed

Your recordings are transcribed and scored by AI models that run on our own servers. We do not send your voice to outside AI companies, and we do not use your recordings to train models for anyone else. If we ever want to use recordings to improve our own scoring, we will ask for your consent first.

4. Voice fingerprint (biometric data) — separate consent

A voice fingerprint is sensitive (biometric) data, so we ask for it separately, it is optional, and the box is not ticked for you. If you say no, you can still use every part of Speakoff; we simply do not run the voice part of the anti-cheat check for you. You can turn it on or off at any time in Settings.

  • Purpose: only to check that you, and not another person or an AI voice, are answering. Never for advertising, never shared, never sold, never used to identify you outside Speakoff.
  • Items: a protected template (a list of numbers made from your voice), not a recording.
  • Retention: your newest 10 templates; all deleted immediately when you withdraw consent or delete your account.
  • No extra audio: the template is made in memory from the answer being scored; no extra copy of your audio is created for it.
  • Irreversible: the template is transformed with a secret key and stored at reduced precision, so it cannot be turned back into your voice or matched elsewhere; changing the key cancels all old templates.
  • Encrypted and separate: encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (HTTPS), kept in a separate database with no names, emails or phone numbers, linked only by a pseudonymous code; keys are stored apart from the data; never included in backups.
  • On your device next: in the coming iPhone and Android apps we plan to create and compare templates on your phone.

5. Automated decisions and your right to a human review

Some results are decided automatically: your scores and levels; who wins a Ranked match and the RP change; the anti-cheat verdict, which can lower a score; and bad-language detection, which can turn an answer into a Ranked loss (a first time is only a warning). A 1-week Ranked ban needs 3 reports from other players. Ranked questions appear only when the match starts, and we check for replayed recordings, word-for-word repeats, AI-written text and (with consent) a voice that is not yours. These decisions only affect results inside Speakoff. You can ask us to review any of them by a person, explain your view, and have it corrected.

6. What other players can see

  • Your username, display name, photo, country flag, rank, skills, statistics and cosmetics.
  • Your friends see whether you are online and what you are doing (for example "in a match").
  • If "Let opponents hear my answer" is on, your opponent can play your Ranked answer, and others may meet a replay of it. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Your email, phone number, normal recordings and voice data are never shown to other players.

7. Who receives data (processors and third parties)

  • Hosting provider in South Korea, where our servers and your data are stored (the company name will be added here when the service goes online).
  • Microsoft text-to-speech: when you press Play on spoken feedback or a mock-test question, the text to be read aloud (it may include short examples from your answer) is sent to Microsoft's voice service. No audio of you is sent.
  • Email provider: to deliver sign-in and password-reset codes.
  • Google / Apple: only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • App Store, Google Play or a card processor for payments when paid plans launch; we never see your full card number.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Processors may use your data only on our instructions, under written contracts. We do not sell personal data.

The EU and the UK recognise South Korea as giving an adequate level of protection (EU adequacy decision of 2021, UK data bridge of 2022); for other countries we use the safeguards your law requires. 9. Retention and destruction

  • Retention for each item is in the table in section 2.
  • Deletion is automatic: old audio after the newest-10 rule, everything when you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), voice templates when you withdraw consent.
  • Files are deleted and database space is cleared so the data cannot be recovered through the app.
  • We keep one encrypted backup of the account database a day for 30 days so it can be restored after a failure; backups never include recordings, photos or voice data, and deleted data leaves them within 30 days.
  • If a law requires us to keep something longer (for example purchase records), we keep only that, separately, for that time.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can: access your data and get a copy in a portable format; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to a use of it; withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect earlier processing); ask for a human review of automated decisions; and complain to a data protection authority. Many of these you can do directly in the app (Settings, Delete account, voice fingerprint and answer-sharing switches); for the rest, write to the contact in section 1. We answer within 30 days (or sooner if your law requires) and may need to confirm your identity. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

  • EU / UK (GDPR): all rights above; you can complain to your local supervisory authority.
  • Korea (PIPA): you can ask to view, correct, delete or suspend processing; you can contact the Personal Information Protection Commission, the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee or the KISA privacy report center (118).
  • Japan (APPI), China (PIPL), India (DPDP Act), Indonesia (PDP Law), Vietnam (Decree 13), Uzbekistan and Russia: you have the rights your law gives, including access, correction, deletion and withdrawal of consent, and can complain to your national authority.
  • California and other US states: you can know, delete and correct your data; we do not sell or share personal data for cross-context advertising.

11. Children

Speakoff is for people aged 14 and over. If you are younger than the age your country requires to agree to this policy yourself (for example under 14 in Korea, under 16 in some EU countries, under 18 in India), a parent or guardian must agree for you. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that a child signed up without the required consent, we delete the account.

12. Device permissions, cookies and local storage

  • Microphone: to record answers. Camera: only in video mode. Notifications: only if you turn them on. You can change these in your browser or phone settings at any time.
  • Cookie: one strictly necessary sign-in cookie (up to 30 days).
  • Local storage on your device: your preferences only (theme, app language, sound, last open tabs, notification setting).
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

13. How we protect your data

  • Passwords stored as salted hashes; HTTPS for all connections when the service is online; sign-in cookies protected against scripts and cross-site use.
  • Voice templates protected, encrypted and kept separately (section 4); backups encrypted, with keys kept apart.
  • Access to servers limited to the people who need it; protection against request forgery and abuse.

No system is perfectly safe. If a breach is likely to affect your rights, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires (for example within 72 hours in the EU).

14. Reports and moderation

If another player reports your Ranked answer for bad language, we record the report. Reports, warnings and bans are kept with your account and are used only for fair play.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way, we will show it in the app and ask you to agree again before you continue. Earlier versions are available on request.

16. Languages

This policy is available in several languages. If they differ, the English version applies, except where your local law requires the version in your language.
